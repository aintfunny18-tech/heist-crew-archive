# Portrait direction

All eight operatives are Black and are designed as members of one visual ensemble: consistent cinematic painterly rendering, dark architectural backgrounds, controlled warm edge lighting, square canonical crops, and a distinct accent color for each role.

Species remains visually legible rather than cosmetic:

- Conman — Changeling; short natural curls, subtly uncanny eyes and symmetry.
- Cleaner — Reborn; silver-gray locs, ritual markings, restrained medical-monastic presentation.
- Hacker — male Rock Gnome; compact proportions, pronounced gnomish features, unruly locs, goggles, soot, and tools.
- Hitter — Bugbear; pronounced muzzle, large ears, fangs, dense fur, and long-limbed power.
- Vent Guy — Wood Elf; practical druid leathers, animal-form tokens, and subtle elemental traces.
- Driver — summer Eladrin; ember-bright eyes, copper-red locs, heat-vein markings, and travel gear.
- Distraction — Fairy; regal presentation, gold jewelry, and luminous amber-gold wings.
- Pickpocket — Lightfoot Halfling; compact proportions, understated clothing, curls, and discreet tools.

One canonical portrait file is reused everywhere in the interface. No separate thumbnail generation is permitted.

Each primary portrait uses a character-specific focal position rather than a universal center crop. The Conman has one approved alternate reveal portrait; the normal portrait remains the default.
